How the Journey Works

Four levels. One verdict. No gut-feel required.

The journey begins the moment you enter a loan scenario in the AI chat — your income, target price, and down payment. From there, each step deepens the analysis. By the time you see a Track 5 score, four independent data sources have been synthesised into a single, honest verdict: Buy, Wait, or Walk Away.

The Four Steps

Each step builds on the last

L1 💬
Step 1 — Scenario Card (35% weight)
L1 — Affordability Signal
Can you actually afford this at today's rate?

You start by telling the AI your income, existing debts, target price, and down payment. HomeRates.AI pulls the live 30-year conforming rate from the Federal Reserve (FRED) and runs a deterministic PITI calculation — not an estimate, not a range, the actual number. Your L1 score reflects how comfortably the payment fits your finances today.

L2 🏠
Step 2 — Check Property (25% weight)
L2 — Market Conditions
Is this a buyer's market or a seller's market right now?

Paste any address or Zillow/Redfin URL into the Scenario Card and hit Check Property. The system scrapes live Redfin data — days on market, sale-to-list ratio, and listing activity — and scores the local market in real time. A DOM above 60 days with sale-to-list below 98% is negotiating room. A DOM under 15 days is a bidding-war market.

L3 📊
Step 3 — Property Intelligence (25% weight)
L3 — Value Gap
Is the list price fair, cheap, or overpriced?

Clicking the Track 5 badge in your Scenario Card opens the full Property Intelligence report. Redfin's Automated Valuation Model (AVM) — built from recent comp sales within 0.5 miles — is compared against the listing price. A gap above 5% in your favour is genuine value. A gap above 12% against you means the seller is anchoring high.

L4 🧠
Step 4 — Full Market Analysis · Deep Mode (15% weight)
L4 — Location Intelligence
Will this neighbourhood support the investment long-term?

Hitting Run Full Market Analysis in the Property Intelligence card triggers Grok-4 deep analysis via the xAI Responses API. Tavily searches the live web across Walk Score, school ratings, commute data, wildfire risk, and 3-year appreciation trends. Grok synthesises all seven sub-factors into a composite Location Intelligence score — the signal most buyers skip, and the one that most determines resale value and quality of life.
