Lead Conversion Pilot: HomeRates alongside your existing lead workflow

Every LendingTree lead is also called by several other lenders, and most never pick up. Speed is already solved by SELA. The remaining gap is giving a rate-shopper a reason to trust Sure First over everyone else calling. HomeRates fills that gap without replacing anything you already run.

One connection point: Bonzo. SELA, RingCentral and Arive stay exactly as they are. HomeRates never calls or texts a borrower itself. Every message still goes out from Bonzo, under Sure First's name and existing consent.

Five ideas, each aimed at a known lead-buyer problem

The problemWhat HomeRates addsNeeds Bonzo access?
Every lender sends the same "call me back" text Personal scenario page. A Sure First–branded page built from the lead's own details: payment, cash to close, county loan limits, FHA vs. conventional, and today's market rate range with a cited source. The text becomes "Here's your scenario at today's rates." No, to start
"Another lender quoted me lower" Quote checker. The borrower enters every quote they've received, Sure First's included. HomeRates compares APR, points cost and break-even against the market benchmark. It's honest, and the LO who offered it becomes the trusted one. No
LOs call back cold, with no context Pre-call brief in Bonzo. When a lead lands, a note appears with the scenario, market range, a likely program fit and suggested talking points. Yes
Leads that aren't ready go cold and buy elsewhere later Rate Watch. The borrower sets their own target payment or rate. When the market reaches it, they get a Sure First–branded alert with one button: "Talk to my loan officer." It keeps aged leads, where most of the spend sits, alive for months. No
Borrowers distrust lead-gen callers Ask-first questions + a no-tracking promise. The borrower can send Sure First structured questions on costs, process and lock policy before committing. HomeRates never reports when a borrower opens a page. The LO hears only what the borrower chooses to send. No

How we'd test it

  1. Weeks 1–2Manual pilot. An LO enters a lead's basics and gets a branded link to paste into their Bonzo text. No integration. 30–50 leads.
  2. Weeks 3–6Connect Bonzo. Scenario page and pre-call brief are created automatically, and the link fills into existing sequences.
  3. Weeks 7–12Full set. Quote checker, Rate Watch and ask-first questions are live on every lead.
Measured in your systems, not ours. Alternate leads get the HomeRates sequence vs. your current one. We compare contact rate, reply rate and applications in Arive. Funded-loan lift takes longer to prove at a ~2% baseline. For example, at 500 leads a month, 2% → 3% is 10 → 15 funded loans.

Our commitments

  • Nothing to replace. Bonzo, SELA, RingCentral and Arive stay as they are.
  • Sure First's brand, NMLS ID and required disclosures on every borrower page, plus HomeRates' educational disclaimer.
  • No credit scores or SSNs stored. Credit fields are dropped on arrival, and borrowers pick their own credit range on the page.
  • No borrower tracking. The LO sees only what the borrower chooses to send.
  • Flat software fee only. Never paid per lead or per loan. To be confirmed with compliance counsel.
  • The test must earn its place. If your LOs don't keep using it on their own by week 2, we change it before building more.

What we need from you

  1. Bonzo: which plan, are the API and webhooks turned on, and who has admin access?
  2. Lead fields: which LendingTree fields come through to Bonzo? A sample with personal details removed would help.
  3. Volume and baseline: leads per month, cost per lead, current contact rate and funded rate.
  4. Pilot team: how many LOs, and who will give honest feedback?
  5. Current messaging: what does your Bonzo text and email sequence say today?
  6. SELA: does it also leave texts or voicemails? We shouldn't double up on messages.
  7. LendingTree terms: the borrower consent wording, and whether lead data can be processed by a third-party tool.
  8. Branding: Sure First logo, NMLS ID and licensed states.
  9. Success: what would make you and your LOs call this a win at 90 days?
