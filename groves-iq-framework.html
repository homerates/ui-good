Section 1
What Each Platform Does

HomeRates.ai and Groves IQ serve the same professional network — mortgage loan officers — but at entirely different points in the loan lifecycle. There is no overlap.

HomeRates.ai — Front of Journey
→
Consumer intelligence platform: AI-powered mortgage scenarios, affordability analysis, property intelligence, rate monitoring
→
Operates before application: consumer is exploring, qualifying, comparing options
→
LO-sponsored model: loan officers invite borrowers, whose experience is branded to the LO
→
Revenue: LO subscription (per borrower or flat monthly)
Groves IQ — Back of Journey
→
AI-powered loan operations: takes underwritten files and manages conditions, stipulations through to funding
→
Operates after approval: file is in-process, conditions being satisfied, CTC in progress
→
ICE/Encompass integration: enterprise connection to 500+ broker network
→
Revenue: LO/broker subscription through Groves IQ platform
Key insight: A consumer who finds a home and qualifies through HomeRates becomes the application that Groves IQ processes. HomeRates creates the borrower; Groves IQ closes the file. The two products are bookends of the same transaction.

Section 3
Proposed Arrangement

This is a distribution partnership, not a technical integration. No shared infrastructure is required at launch. The arrangement has two components:

Primary
Component A — Distribution
Groves IQ distributes HomeRates to its 500+ LO network as a value-added tool. LOs get HomeRates bundled into their Groves IQ subscription, or at a negotiated group rate.
What Groves IQ LOs get: HomeRates Pro LO access — ability to invite borrowers, view their scenarios, and have their branding on the consumer experience.

What Groves IQ gets: Stronger LO retention (additional value in the platform), and a differentiated offering vs competitors.
Secondary
Component B — Referral Pipeline
HomeRates consumer base — borrowers who are application-ready — can be referred to Groves IQ LOs as a pipeline channel.
What Groves IQ LOs get: Referrals of pre-qualified, scenario-ready borrowers who have been through the full HomeRates discovery process.

What HomeRates gets: A professional network to refer consumers into — completing the end-to-end journey.
Why this is clean: Neither party is dependent on the other's core product. HomeRates runs with or without Groves IQ. Groves IQ runs with or without HomeRates. The arrangement adds value without creating risk for either side.
Section 4 — New
Planned UX/UI Changes — Consumer Experience

In parallel with this partnership discussion, HomeRates is building a dedicated consumer surface — a lighter, property-first experience designed for borrowers who arrive without a loan officer. This is directly relevant to the Groves IQ partnership because it defines the product their LOs will be sponsoring for their borrowers.

The consumer experience follows a single design principle: same data as the Pro platform, rendered through a consumer lens. Simpler language, fewer controls, faster path from question to answer. No mortgage jargon. No page navigation — everything flows inside a single chat thread.

Core design principles across all consumer surfaces
→Address search as the hero entry point
→Zero page navigation — all in chat
→Hero number first, context second
→2 adjusters max, not a slider forest
→Plain-English verdict, not raw data
→One primary CTA per card

Four interactive prototypes have been built and are linked below. These represent the planned consumer UI — not speculative designs. They are the basis for the React implementation that follows.

Commercial Terms — Draft Framework
Term Proposed Structure Notes
Distribution model Groves IQ bundles HomeRates LO access into their LO subscription Either at cost or at a negotiated group rate per active LO
Pricing to Groves IQ To be negotiated — volume pricing Suggested: flat per-LO/mo or revenue share on LOs who activate borrowers
Referral component To be defined Could be referral fee per funded loan, or simply goodwill / LO availability
Branding LO's name/company on consumer experience. "Powered by HomeRates" attribution. Groves IQ branding optional — consumer sees their LO, not the platform
Data Consumer data stays in HomeRates. Scenario data shared with LO only (their own borrower). No consumer behavioral data shared with Groves IQ platform per HomeRates privacy model
Integration None required at launch. Future: optional Encompass/ICE data bridge. Phase 1 is purely distribution — no technical dependencies
Term 12 months initial, auto-renewing Either party can exit with 60 days notice
