Navigation Drawer — Before / After

Current drawer has up to 15 items across 3 sections. Proposed: 4 primary links + collapsible secondary.

Why this change?

The current hamburger drawer contains: 5 main nav links + 6 quick links + admin section + credits display. On mobile, users scanning for "My Properties" have to visually parse up to 15 items. The primary actions (Chat, My Home, Messages, My Vault) cover 90% of usage. Everything else is secondary. The proposed change creates a clear two-tier structure: 4 primary links always visible, everything else behind a "More" expander. Credits and admin stay but move to a footer strip.
