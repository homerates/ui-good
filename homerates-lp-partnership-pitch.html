The solution
Track 5 — buyer intelligence embedded
directly inside the agent's website.

HomeRates' Track 5 engine embeds as a widget on any LP property page. When a buyer browses a listing, they instantly see their Decision Score, real monthly payment, and loan options — all within the agent's branded experience.

1
Decision Score on every listing
A 0–100 buyer readiness score appears on the property detail page — income, savings, market timing, and credit factors — specific to that home's price point.

FHA vs conventional vs jumbo — side by side with real numbers. The buyer understands their options before they ever pick up the phone.
