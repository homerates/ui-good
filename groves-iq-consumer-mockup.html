The model

How value flows between all three parties
LO
Pays once, retains forever
The loan officer's subscription unlocks premium features for every client they invite. Their name stays in front of the borrower between transactions — exactly like Homebot, but with live mortgage intelligence instead of just home values.
0¢
Consumer always free
The borrower never pays. They get a better product because their LO is on the platform. The unlock mechanic creates a natural incentive for consumers to work with a HomeRates-connected loan officer.
2×
Groves IQ gets both sides
Their 500+ LOs get a borrower engagement tool. Those borrowers become HomeRates users. As HomeRates grows its own consumer base, Groves IQ LOs have access to that pipeline too. Both platforms grow together.
