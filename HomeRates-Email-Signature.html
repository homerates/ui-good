1

Select everything inside the green box below — click inside it, press Ctrl+A (Win) or Cmd+A (Mac), then Ctrl+C to copy.

2

Log in to privateemail.com → click ⚙ Settings → Identities → click your email address.

3

In the Signature editor, click the <> (Source/HTML) button in the toolbar to switch to HTML mode.

4

Delete any existing content, paste the copied content, click <> again to go back to visual mode, then Save.

5

If you see a "HTML signature" checkbox near the signature field — make sure it's ticked.
