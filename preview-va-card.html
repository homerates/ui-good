💰 VA Residual Income Test (VA Pamphlet 26-7)
After paying PITI, debts, and maintenance, VA requires leftover (residual) income for family expenses. This secondary qualifier often allows higher DTIs when residual income is strong — and is why VA approvals differ from conventional underwriting.
