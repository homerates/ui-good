### Competitive read — 19 companies on the [demos page](https://digital-mortgage.nationalmortgagenews.com/demos/) (checked 2026-08-07)

All lender/LO-side B2B operational tools — **none are consumer-facing decision
support**, which is the clean differentiator to lead with at the actual demo:

| Category | Companies |
|---|---|
| LOS/pipeline AI copilots for LOs | Copperlane ("Penny"), inflooens ("Luna"), Insyde.ai, Jazzx.ai |
| Document automation/compliance | Automat, DocMagic, Ocrolus, ACI (appraisal) |
| CRM/sales tools for lenders | Insellerate ("Aithena"), Alpharun, Verse.ai |
| Servicing/payments/back-office | Dara by Sagent, REPAY, UiPath, Mezzo, LoNav Solutions |
| Data/enterprise | Cotality (property data), CI&T (IT consulting) |

**Closest overlap:** MMI One — "unifies market intelligence, CRM automation,
and borrower monitoring." Still a lender growth/CRM platform sold to
lenders; "borrower monitoring" means lenders watching borrowers, not
borrowers getting their own decision support.

**Positioning takeaway for the actual demo/booth pitch:** every other
company here makes the *lender* faster (documents, underwriting, CRM
follow-up, AI copilots for LOs). None gives the *borrower* — the person
actually making the decision — usable information first. Lead with that
contrast directly rather than "we're an AI mortgage tool too."
