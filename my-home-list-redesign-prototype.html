What's changing
1
Ordering: most recently active first, not creation order. Was: sorted by is_primary, then oldest-created-first — a property added 10 minutes ago could land behind ones from weeks ago, off in "+N More ▾".
2
Delete (×) is now on every visible chip, revealed on hover — not just inside the overflow drawer. Was: the × delete button only existed on chips already hidden behind "More ▾" — the first 3 visible chips had no way to remove them at all.
3
A newly analyzed property jumps to the front and becomes active immediately — same command bar, same "Analyze →" button, no new step. Was: new properties were appended to the end of the list, so a 4th+ property was invisible without opening "More ▾" first.
4
Re-entering an address you already have switches to it and says so (notice banner above) — this part already shipped today, included here to show the full flow together.
5
Search bar stays the fixed first line — unchanged from the current page, confirmed correct as-is.
6
The "Welcome back" quicklink to /activity stays up top, directly under the command bar — confirmed to keep, not remove, in the reorg.
