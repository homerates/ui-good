The Problem

Home search and mortgage are broken —
and nobody connects the two

Zillow and Redfin show you beautiful listings — but can't tell you if you can actually afford the home, what the true PITI will be, or whether the market is overheated.

No platform combines property intelligence + mortgage math into a single unbiased score. Buyers make $500K decisions switching between broken, conflicted tools.

The result: The industry profits from the knowledge gap — leaving borrowers confused, worn down, and left wondering whether they got the best deal or the right loan terms.

Platform Strategy · AI-Native By Design

Built to be invoked inside ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, and Gemini —
not locked to one platform's roadmap

Consumer search behavior is repeating the shift it made with Google twenty years ago — except faster. People now start with AI, and only venture outside it for additional value the AI itself can't supply. AI-platform strategy is evolving in real time; betting on one vendor's proprietary plugin ecosystem is a structural risk. HomeRates is built on MCP — the open, cross-platform protocol multiple leading AI systems are converging on — so our growth compounds with the AI market itself, not against any single platform's roadmap.

Product · Track 5 Decision Intelligence

Five levels of decision intelligence —
from property evaluation to rate decoding

Context is what turns data into a clear choice. Four weighted levels compute one honest Decision Score; a fifth, Rate Intelligence, decodes the exact rate a lender should quote — kept separate so a rate quote can never inflate the underlying verdict.

Level 1
Financial Readiness
35%
Deterministic LTV formula by loan type — Conventional, FHA, VA, Jumbo. No guessing.
Level 2
Property Evaluation
25%
PITI gap analysis, AVM valuation comparison, days on market, income qualification score.
Level 3
Market Intelligence
25%
Median DOM, sale-to-list ratio, comp velocity — live data via Tavily search + Grok reasoning.
Level 4
Location Fit
15%
7 dimensions: schools, crime, transit, walkability, job proximity, flood zone, wildfire risk.
Weighted Composite: L1×35% + L2×25% + L3×25% + L4×15% → One honest verdict before you make an offer
Rate Intelligence
A fifth, separately-surfaced capability — decodes where a quoted lender rate ranks against real OBMMI market segments, calculated from live FRED data + the public Fannie Mae LLPA matrix. Deliberately kept OUT of the weighted composite above, so a rate quote can never inflate or deflate the underlying buy/wait/walk-away verdict.
3 entry points: My Home · Check Property · Paste any Redfin/Zillow URL in chat — Rate Intelligence runs separately at chat.homerates.ai/rate-intelligence-engine

Business Model · Platform Licensing

The entire professional side, white-labeled — a second SaaS revenue stream

Enterprise isn't a support tier bolted onto the consumer product — it's a distinct SaaS licensing business. Everything a loan officer or broker sees on HomeRates today (Decision Score, Rate Engine, DSCR/portfolio tools, the LO dashboard) can be re-skinned under a partner's own name, logo, and brand color, then deployed as their consumer-facing tool. One retail Pro seat is $19/mo; one enterprise deployment can carry an entire brokerage or lender's book of loan officers under a single custom contract.

Already Live, Not Speculative

The white-label infrastructure is shipped and running in production today — partner branding (logo, tagline, accent color), a fully working consumer flow at a partner-specific URL. First deployment: Sure First Lending.
