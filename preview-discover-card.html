My HomeRates scenario shows an FHA loan of $550,760 at 6.30% with zero points. What rate are you quoting me, and does it include any discount points?
HomeRates AI
Verified answer: The FRED 30-year average is 6.30% as of Apr 30, 2026. That's the zero-point benchmark. Any quote above 6.55% with no points attached warrants a follow-up — ask the lender to confirm their Section A origination charge.
Your Loan Officer
I'm quoting you 6.375% with 0 points. My origination fee is $8,260 which is 1.5% — that covers my processing, underwriting, and lock fee.
⚠️
Discover gap detected: The quoted rate of 6.375% is within normal range. However, $8,260 in Section A fees is 1.5% of loan — above the typical 0.5–1.0% range. Ask the lender to itemize what's inside that charge. Processing and underwriting are normally $800–$1,200 combined. The remaining ~$6,000 should be explained line by line.
You · via Discover
What is my total cash to close on this purchase, including all prepaids and escrow setup?
HomeRates AI
Verified estimate: $36,460 = $19,632 down + ~$16,828 closing costs (3% of price). Closing costs at 3% include origination, title, escrow, and prepaids. If the lender quotes above $41,000, ask which line items are driving the difference — specifically tax/insurance assumptions and escrow reserves.
Your Loan Officer
My estimate is $41,200 total cash to close. The higher number is due to my origination fee and a 1.35% property tax assumption for your area.
✅
Plausible explanation: A 1.35% tax rate adds ~$570/yr extra in escrow vs the 1.1% HomeRates default — that's ~$1,140 more in reserves at close. Combined with the higher origination fee, the $41,200 total is consistent with what the lender described. The origination fee is still worth negotiating.
