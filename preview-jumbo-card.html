Educational estimates only. A "jumbo" loan is any loan exceeding the 2026 FHFA conforming baseline of $832,750 for a 1-unit residence in a standard-cost area. High-balance areas (designated high-cost counties) have limits up to $1,249,125. Loans between $832,750 and the county high-balance limit may qualify as "agency jumbo" (backed by Fannie/Freddie) with different pricing than true non-conforming jumbo. Loans above the county high-balance limit are held in portfolio by lenders. Property tax estimated at 1.1%/yr; insurance at 0.5%/yr.
