The Problem

Home search and mortgage are broken —
and nobody connects the two

🏠 Home Tools Know the Property

Zillow and Redfin show you beautiful listings — but can't tell you if you can actually afford the home, what the true PITI will be, or whether the market is overheated.

💰 Mortgage Tools Sell You Leads

Bankrate and LendingTree show "rates" that are paid lender placements. Every inquiry triggers 4–7 lender calls. Your data is the product — not the advice.

🔗 Nobody Bridges the Gap

No platform combines property intelligence + mortgage math into a single unbiased score. Buyers make $500K decisions switching between broken, conflicted tools.

The result: Consumers fall in love with a home they can't afford, get sold a loan on the wrong terms, and nobody is accountable — because everyone profits from the gap.
